Blossom Light

The original wall-shadow photograph is served by the AtomicMotion web demo at
https://atomicmotion.dev/textures/wall-shadow.jpg. The Swift component loads
that existing URL and accepts a caller-provided image URL. The photographer,
original source, and redistribution terms have not been confirmed. See the
project's ASSETS.md before redistributing the reference photo.

The bundled Metal shader's procedural leaf-shadow fallback and the SwiftUI
preview recorded with that fallback are original AtomicMotion artwork.

Manrope Regular and SemiBold are static instances of the Manrope variable font.
See Manrope-OFL.txt in the package's Design resources.
